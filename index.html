Section 01 · The foundation

Three points. One personality.

Today we focus on the Big 3: the Sun, Moon, and Ascendant. This is the foundation of the natal chart and the first level of analysis. These three elements describe identity, emotional responses, and the way a person presents themselves to the world.

Luminaries

Sun & Moon

The two main sources of light in the chart. They define the core of personality and inner life. Always analyzed first — without them, deeper analysis loses context.

The cusp

Ascendant

Not a planet, but the cusp of the 1st house. It marks the beginning of the chart and shapes how a person enters and reacts to the outer world.

Sign · House · Aspects — the three lenses. The sign shows how the energy is expressed. The house shows where it manifests in life. The aspects show how it interacts with other planets. A single layer hints at the energy. All three layers together give a precise reading.
A worked example — Sun in three layers

Take one Sun position and see what each layer adds:

Sun in Capricorn — a goal-oriented, disciplined identity.
Sun in the 10th house — that identity is built through career and public image.
Sun square Saturn — pressure on the self, high inner demands.
Together → ambition channelled through career, with a strong inner critic to manage.

Identity, direction, and the will to act

Section 03 · Moon

Emotion, comfort, and the inner child

The Moon represents the emotional world and subconscious patterns. It shows how a person feels, reacts, and what creates a sense of safety. It reflects the inner child and early conditioning — and defines how a person restores energy and handles stress.

Section 04 · Ascendant

First contact, not full character

The Ascendant is not a planet — it is the cusp of the 1st house and marks the beginning of the chart. It shapes appearance, body language, and the way a person enters new situations. It is the outer layer that is visible before deeper traits are revealed.

Section 05 · Practice

Theory holds. Practice makes it stick.

Five short exercises. They get progressively more demanding — from quick recognition to a full personality synthesis. Take them in order. Skip nothing.

A note before you start. Adult learning sticks when it is active: you retrieve information instead of re-reading it, you connect new ideas to your own life, and you receive immediate feedback. That is what this section is for. Pen and notebook optional but recommended.
1
Active recall · 2 min

Quick check — do the basics hold?

Three short questions. Pick the answer you believe most. Feedback appears immediately — wrong answers teach as much as right ones.

Q1.Which two points are called the Luminaries?
The Luminaries are the Sun and Moon. They are the two main sources of light in the chart and are always analysed first. The Ascendant is not a planet — it is the cusp of the 1st house.
Q2."How do I feel and what do I need to feel safe?" — which point answers this?
The Moon. It rules emotional needs, comfort, sense of safety, and the inner child. The Sun answers "who am I?", the Ascendant answers "how do I appear?".
Q3.You read a chart only by the Ascendant sign and stop there. What did you miss?
Planets in the 1st house. Mars in the 1st adds intensity and directness; Venus in the 1st softens the image and adds charm. The Ascendant opens the door — the planets walk through it.

Model interpretation

A is closest. Sun in Capricorn / 10th = identity built through career and visible achievement. Moon in Cancer / 4th = emotional needs are met at home, near family, in soft rituals. Ascendant in Libra = the social opening is gracious, balanced, easy to like.

Reading the three together: ambition outside, tenderness inside, charm at the door. None of these contradict — they are three layers of the same person.
